# Privacy

Crate Bell collects nothing. The full policy, also used for the Chrome Web Store, is at <https://gregoiremalthes.github.io/crate-bell/privacy.html> (source: [docs/privacy.html](docs/privacy.html)).

In short:

- Preferences and the "are alert emails on?" status are stored locally in your browser and deleted with the extension.
- Requests go only to www.discogs.com (pages you open, your notification settings page) and api.discogs.com (public list of an album's pressings).
- No analytics, no ads, no remote code, no data shared with anyone.
