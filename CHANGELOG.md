# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (unreleased)

- Switches on the guide page to turn off record page buttons, wantlist buttons, album cards or the alert page guide.
- Links to your saved alerts and your wantlist on Discogs from the guide page.
- Guide drawings use Daft Punk - Homework as the example record.

## 1.1.0 (unreleased)

- "Sale alert on" status on record pages once an alert exists (paused when the record left the wantlist), with guided deletion.
- "Set alert" button and "Alert on" tag on every record of the wantlist page.
- Crate Bell remembers its alerts and keeps that list in step with Discogs' Saved Searches menu.

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

First version.

- Pink "Set a sale alert" button on record pages, adding the record to the wantlist when needed.
- "Any pressing" alerts on album (master) pages.
- Guide on Discogs' "Items I Want" page: points at Save Search, fills in the name and email frequency, explains Discogs' limits and errors, confirms the alert.
- Check of the Discogs email setting alerts depend on, with a highlight on the settings page.
- Usual filters set once: condition, sleeve, generic sleeves, seller rating, ships from.
- Popup and guide page. English, French, German.
