Privacy policy

Short version: Crate Bell collects nothing.

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Applies to the Crate Bell browser extension.

What Crate Bell stores

On your own computer only, in the browser's extension storage:

  • your preferences (email frequency, record and sleeve condition, countries, seller rating, formats chosen on album pages);
  • whether your Discogs sale alert emails are on or off, and when this was last checked;
  • for up to ten minutes, the record you are setting an alert for, so the alert page can be filled in;
  • how many alerts you have set with Crate Bell.

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What Crate Bell sends, and where

  • www.discogs.com: Crate Bell runs on Discogs pages you open, and reads your Discogs notification settings page (with your own Discogs session, like your browser does) to tell you if sale alert emails are off. It clicks Discogs' own "Add to Wantlist" button only when you ask it to.
  • api.discogs.com: on album pages, Crate Bell asks Discogs' public API for the list of pressings. No login and no personal data are involved.

Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There are no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no remote code. Fonts are bundled with the extension.

Data sold or shared

None. Crate Bell does not sell, transfer or use any data for purposes unrelated to its single purpose: helping you set up Discogs sale alerts.

When you uninstall

Chrome opens a short goodbye page on this site. Nothing about you is added to its address.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.

Crate Bell is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Discogs.
