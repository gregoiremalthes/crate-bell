## Description (en)

Never miss a record you want.

Crate Bell adds one pink button to every record page on Discogs. Click it, and Discogs emails you as soon as a copy goes on sale.

BEFORE YOU START: TURN ON ONE SETTING
Discogs sends sale alerts by email only. In your Discogs notification settings, tick "Personal Email" on the line "When a release in my wantlist is listed for sale", then save:
https://www.discogs.com/settings/notifications
Crate Bell checks this setting for you and tells you if it is off.

HOW IT WORKS

1. On any record page, click "Set a sale alert" (or "Alert me when it's for sale" if the record is not in your wantlist yet: Crate Bell adds it first).
2. Discogs' alert page opens with your record and your usual filters already selected. A pink ring shows you where to click.
3. Crate Bell names the alert and picks how often you want emails. You click Save. Done.

ANY PRESSING WILL DO?
On an album's main page (Discogs calls it a master release), set one alert for every pressing you would accept, for example all vinyl versions.

YOUR USUAL FILTERS, SET ONCE
Email right away or once a day, minimum record and sleeve condition, no generic sleeves, seller rating, ships from (with a one-click European Union choice).

HOW ALERTS STOP
Alerts only cover records in your wantlist. When you buy a record and add it to your collection from the feedback screen, it leaves your wantlist and the emails stop. To delete an alert yourself: open "Items I Want", pick it in "Saved Searches", click Delete. Crate Bell shows you where.

MADE FOR PEOPLE, NOT FOR POWER USERS ONLY
Large buttons, plain words, step-by-step guidance, in English, French and German.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no analytics. Crate Bell only talks to Discogs, with your own session. Your preferences stay on your computer. Open source: https://github.com/gregoiremalthes/crate-bell

FREE
Crate Bell is free. If it saves you time, you can buy me a coffee: https://ko-fi.com/gregoirep

Crate Bell is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Discogs.

## Privacy tab

**Single purpose:** Helps Discogs users set up Discogs' own email sale alerts for records in their wantlist, by adding a button to record pages and guiding them through Discogs' alert page.

**Permission justifications**

- `storage`: saves the user's alert preferences (email frequency, conditions, countries) and whether Discogs alert emails are on, locally on the device.
- Host `https://www.discogs.com/*`: shows the button on Discogs record and album pages, guides the user on Discogs' "Items I Want" page, and reads the user's Discogs notification settings page to tell them if sale alert emails are switched off.
- Remote code: none. All code ships in the package.

**Data usage:** The extension does not collect or transmit any user data. Certify: no sale of data, no use unrelated to the single purpose, no creditworthiness use.

**Privacy policy URL:** https://gregoiremalthes.github.io/crate-bell/privacy.html
