# 5. Highlighter design system, calm and didactic

Date: 2026-11-24 · Status: accepted

## Decision

- Tokens from the Highlighter system: white paper, highlighter pink `#f69cc6` for the primary action and discs, black ink, 2px black strokes, square cards, 8px controls, no shadows.
- Fraunces (soft, light) for titles, DM Sans for text. Text on pink is always black (10.5:1).
- "Cute" comes from line art (a record with a bell, a crate) and warm, plain copy, not from emoji or exclamation marks.
- Teaching patterns: one pink ring on the next thing to click, numbered steps with a pink current step, drawings of Discogs pages in the guide, "Saved" confirmations, plain-language error messages.
- No Discogs logo or look-alike: the brand is "Crate Bell", described as "for Discogs", with "Not affiliated with Discogs." everywhere.
