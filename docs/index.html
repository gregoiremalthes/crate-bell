Before you start: one setting

Discogs sends sale alerts by email only. In your Discogs notification settings, tick "Personal Email" on the line "When a release in my wantlist is listed for sale", then save. Crate Bell checks it for you.

How it works

  1. On any record page, click the pink button. If the record is not in your wantlist yet, Crate Bell adds it first.
  2. Discogs' alert page opens with your record and your usual filters selected. A pink ring shows where to click.
  3. Crate Bell names the alert and picks how often you want emails. You click Save.

Alerts stop on their own once the record leaves your wantlist, for example when you add it to your collection after buying it.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Crate Bell only talks to Discogs, with your own session, and keeps your preferences on your computer. Read the privacy policy or the source code.
